LAXCOMMUTE

Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

LAXCommute is a free, independent app for LAX employee shuttle riders. It keeps as little about you as it can. This page explains what is stored, where, and how to remove it.

On your device

  • As a guest, your commute, theme and language settings stay in your browser.
  • Your location is used on your device to find nearby stops. It is not saved to your account or sent to LAXCommute's servers.
  • Signing out does not remove settings stored on this device.

Stored with Supabase

  • Anonymous crowd reports: bus ID, crowd level and time. They are shown to riders for 15 minutes.
  • Anonymous stop observations: route, stop, what was seen (arrived, departed or crowding) and time.
  • Optional feedback you send to the developer. Only the developer can read it.
  • If you create an account: your email, a password hash and your saved commute. You can delete your account at any time from the Account tab.

Abuse protection and analytics

  • Hashed network identifiers are used to limit abusive requests.
  • If enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits without analytics cookies.

Other services

  • Map providers receive requests for the area of the map you view.
  • Shuttle times come from LAX's shuttle operator. Opening the official tracker takes you to their site, which has its own privacy policy.

Questions

Use "Send feedback to the developer" in the app menu.
